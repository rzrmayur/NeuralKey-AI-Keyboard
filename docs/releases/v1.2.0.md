## NeuralKey AI Keyboard v1.2.0 — Adaptive Key Memory, Foldable & One-Handed Postures, Email AI Suite & Modernized Dashboard

### 🧠 Adaptive On-Device Key Memory & Intelligent Predictions
- **On-Device Key Memory (`KeyMemoryStore` + `SuggestionEngine`):** Automatically learns your personal vocabulary, proper nouns, custom casing, and phrase frequencies purely on-device (never synced to the cloud and strictly disabled in password, email, and URL fields).
- **Contextual N-Gram Next-Word Predictions:** Suggests the top 3 likely next words immediately after pressing Space based on built-in conversational/professional bigrams and your learned typing transitions.
- **QWERTY Spatial Typo Proximity Ranking:** Prioritizes same-length 1-character substitution candidates whose keys are physically adjacent on the QWERTY grid.
- **Interactive Vocabulary Controls:** Teach custom words/names or wipe learned Key Memory with 1 tap inside the new **Smart Typing** tab.

### 📱 One-Handed Mode & Foldable / Flip Device Postures
- **One-Handed Compact Dock (`LEFT` / `RIGHT`):** Compacts the keyboard to `82%` width anchored to either thumb side, with an `18%` quick-action dock sidebar to flip sides (`◀` / `▶`) or expand back to full width (`⤢`).
- **Foldable Unfolded Ergonomic Split Layout:** Automatically detects unfolded inner displays and tablets (`width >= 600dp`) and splits the key grid into left and right thumb clusters with a central ergonomic gap and dual spacebars. Includes an inline split-toggle icon on the command bar.
- **Flip Cover Screen Compact Posture:** Automatically compacts row heights on short cover screens and flex-mode windows (`height <= 440dp`) so the host text field remains visible.
- **Resizable Window Continuity:** Added `android:resizeableActivity="true"`, `PROPERTY_COMPAT_ALLOW_RESIZEABLE_ACTIVITY`, and `smallestScreenSize` configuration handling for seamless fold/unfold transitions.

### ✉️ 32 Built-In AI Actions (New Email Drafts & Smart Writing Suites)
- **Email & Drafts Suite (6 Tools):** *Reply: Confirm & Accept*, *Reply: Polite Decline*, *Cold Outreach Email*, *Follow-Up Nudge*, *Meeting Recap & Actions*, and *Subject Line Generator*. Automatically activates **✉️ Email Mode** when opened inside Gmail, Outlook, or other email apps.
- **Smart Writing & Engineering Suite (4 Tools):** *Expand & Elaborate*, *Brainstorm 5 Angles*, *Pros, Cons & Risks*, and *Bug / Jira Spec*, plus *Translate to German*.
- **Custom AI Action Builder:** Create and delete your own persistent AI tools by category directly from the companion app.

### 🎨 15 Flagship Themes & Modernized Animated Dashboard
- **6 New Curated Themes (15 Total):** *Dracula Pro* 🧛, *Nordic Frost* ❄️, *Mocha Espresso* ☕, *Carbon Titanium* 🏎️, *Abyssal Biolume* 🌊, and *Rose Quartz Studio* 🌹.
- **Redesigned Material 3 Glassmorphic Companion App:** Pulsing live status pill, interactive telemetry strip, spring-animated tab transitions (`AnimatedContent`), interactive key-physics theme preview mockup, and live haptic amplitude preview.
