## 🌟 Headline Enhancements in `v1.3.0`

### 1. 🪄 Ghost-Text Inline AI Autocomplete & Per-App AI Personas (`AppPersonaEngine`)
- **Host-Aware Persona Auto-Detection:** Automatically detects the foreground host application and adapts AI tone, formatting, and Ghost-Text sentence completion:
  - ✉️ **Email Mode (`EMAIL_EXECUTIVE`):** Gmail, Outlook, Thunderbird, ProtonMail, Spark, FairEmail, K-9 Mail.
  - 💬 **Chat Mode (`INSTANT_MESSENGER`):** WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Discord, Instagram DMs, Google Messages.
  - 💼 **Workspace Mode (`WORKSPACE_PRO`):** Slack, Microsoft Teams, Notion, Linear, Jira, Trello.
  - 💻 **Dev Mode (`DEVELOPER_CODE`):** GitHub, GitLab, Termux, Acode, Replit.
  - 🔥 **Social Mode (`SOCIAL_CREATOR`):** X / Twitter, LinkedIn, Reddit, Threads, Bluesky.
  - 🧠 **Universal AI (`UNIVERSAL_NEURAL`):** Balanced adaptive intelligence across all other apps.
- **1-Tap Ghost Complete (`🪄 Ghost Complete`):** Completes your unfinished sentence or paragraph inline while preserving your exact opening words.

### 2. ⚡ Neural Trail Glide / Swipe-to-Type with Neon Trail Physics
- **Continuous Gesture Word Decoding (`SuggestionEngine.decodeGlidePath` & `TypingController.glide`):** Slide across character keys to decode full words 100% offline using anchor start/end matching, ordered subsequence scoring, QWERTY spatial adjacency, and your personal **Key Memory** unigrams/bigrams.
- **Multi-Layered Neon Comet Trail (`KeyboardGridView`):** Renders a smooth quadratic Bézier glowing trail (`outer halo` + `mid energy beam` + `inner white core` + `comet head orb`) dynamically tinted to your active theme's primary and secondary accents.

### 3. 📋 Smart Clipboard Time-Machine & AI Context Fusion (`ClipboardHistoryManager` & `ClipboardFusionPanel`)
- **On-Device Clipboard Ring Buffer:** Stores up to 25 recent non-sensitive clipboard items (strictly respecting Android 13+ `ClipDescription.EXTRA_IS_SENSITIVE` so passwords are never captured), with 1-tap **Pin/Unpin**, **Delete**, and **Clear Unpinned**.
- **AI Context Fusion (`💬 AI Reply`):** Combines a copied incoming message from your clipboard with your current draft notes to generate a complete, context-aware reply in 1 tap—plus 1-tap **📝 TL;DR** and **🌐 Translate** on any clipboard clip.

### 4. ⚔️ Multi-Model Arena Split-Compare (`ArenaComparePanel`)
- **Parallel Dual-LLM Streaming (`OpenRouterRepository.streamModelDirect`):** Streams **2 OpenRouter models side-by-side in real time** inside the keyboard, displaying live token generation, per-model latency (`ms`), and **`✓ Use Model A` / `✓ Use Model B`** winner selection buttons.

### 5. 💻 In-Keyboard Slash Commands & Dynamic Offline Macros (`SlashCommandEngine`)
- **Zero-Latency Offline Macros (Triggered on `Space` or `Enter`):**
  - `/calc <expression>` — Safe recursive-descent math parser supporting `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, `%`, `^`, decimals, and nested parentheses (e.g., `Total: /calc (12 + 8) * 3.5` $\rightarrow$ `Total: 70`).
  - `/date`, `/time`, `/datetime`, `/now` — Expands to current ISO date/time.
  - `/clip` — Expands to your most recent clipboard item.
  - `/shrug` ($\rightarrow$ `¯\_(ツ)_/¯`) and `/tableflip` ($\rightarrow$ `(╯°□°）╯︵ ┻━┻`).
- **Instant AI Slash Commands (Prefix or Suffix, Triggered on `Space` or `Enter`):**
  - `/email`, `/fix`, `/tldr`, `/polite`, `/sql`, `/regex`, `/code`, `/translate` — Automatically strips the slash command token and streams the requested transformation directly in the keyboard.
- **Live Slash Command Autocomplete:** Typing `/` in any text field surfaces matching slash commands directly in the suggestion bar.

### 6. 🎙️ Whisper / Multimodal Voice-to-Action Studio (`VoiceToActionPanel`)
- **Speak Messy Thoughts $\rightarrow$ Structured Output:** Dictate naturally via Android `SpeechRecognizer` (with 1-tap microphone permission flow and sample voice brain-dumps) or OpenRouter `input_audio` WAV streaming (`google/gemini-2.5-flash`, `google/gemini-2.0-flash-001`, `google/gemini-2.0-flash-lite-001`) and transform into:
  - `✨ Smart Polish` (removes filler words & fixes punctuation)
  - `✉️ Voice → Email` (complete professional email)
  - `📋 Voice → Bullets` (actionable bullet list)
  - `🌐 Voice → English` (live translation + polish)
  - `✍️ Raw Text` (direct dictation)

### 7. 🛡️ Hardened OpenRouter Error Diagnostics & Automatic Free-Tier Fallback
- **Actionable HTTP Diagnostics (`OpenRouterApiClient.parseApiError`):** Clear, human-readable error messages for `400`, `401/403` (invalid API key), `402` (credits exhausted), `404` (model offline), `408` (timeout), `429` (rate limit), and `5xx` (provider overload), plus mid-stream SSE error chunk detection.
- **Automatic Emergency Free-Model Recovery (`OpenRouterRepository`):** If your selected model fails with a retriable status (`402` / `404` / `429` / `5xx`) before emitting tokens, NeuralKey automatically retries across `openrouter/free` and `:free` fallback models so your workflow never stalls.
